How this stays fresh. Trade press, newswires and a Google News sweep of every house on the watchlist are polled every five minutes by a server-side job — nothing depends on a laptop or a chat session being open. Every publication date comes from the publisher's own feed metadata, never inferred. A new deal is pushed to your phone once, as soon as it is classified; the same deal from other outlets is merged into it, not re-sent. The Latest holds the past 48 hours; the archive keeps days two through seven. The status line above says when the last sweep finished — if it turns red, the pipe is down, not the market quiet.
